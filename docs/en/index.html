The headquarters of your home and your work, with an assistant that does only what you ask.

A Jarvis-style assistant coordinates a small team of AI agents, keeps an eye on mail and deadlines, connects to the programs you use at work. Next to it, a Home Assistant dashboard designed from scratch: the house on its holographic floor plan, a card for every device, energy read like a bill.

The home

The dashboard Home Assistant deserved

The rooms are Home Assistant's areas, drawn on your floor plan: upload it as a photo or PDF and the AI suggests the rooms, you refine them with your finger. Tap a room and its card opens; commands go straight to Home Assistant, with no AI and no cost.

  • Upload the floor planA photo, a scan or a PDF of your home, several floors too. It becomes the background you draw on.
  • The AI suggests the roomsWith "Suggest rooms" the AI recognises the spaces and matches them to Home Assistant's areas. You move the corners with your finger and fix them.
  • Indoors and outdoorsBalconies and gardens become outlines or go under the plan, in "Outdoors".
  • It becomes a hologramSave and the plan turns into the 3D map of the Home view: lights on, temperatures and open windows at a glance.
  • A card for everythingLights with brightness, climate with its dial, blinds drawn, media with cover art and volume, plugs with their consumption, sensors with the 24-hour chart.
  • Your own cardsBuild a card from several entities: the stove with its on and off times and "automatic", which says by itself "on until 22:00".
  • Times and settingsHome Assistant's helpers (times, numbers, lists) are changed from here: your automations stay where they are.
  • All from HubNEXOrder, rename, choose groups and sizes without touching Home Assistant.
Energy

The bill, before the bill

Three ways to read consumption, one or all of them. A single rate, your own time bands from a sensor, or the Italian F1, F2 and F3 bands computed by HubNEX, holidays included.

  • The reactorThe period's consumption around the power right now, as a share of your meter.
  • The dayToday's power hour by hour: you see when you use energy, and how much.
  • The billWhat you have spent, what you will spend at today's pace, what changed since the last period.
The team

One assistant, many specialists

In agents.yaml you describe your businesses, departments and agents. The assistant delegates: mail to the mail agent, technical research to the technician, quotes to whoever handles them. The 3D headquarters shows who is working, the neural map what the team has learnt.

Telegram

A group with the assistant and the mail agent. Write or send a voice note, they answer by voice too.

Mail with drafts

Emails are sorted by priority; every reply is a draft you approve. Nothing goes out by itself.

The programs of your work

Management software, calendars, CRM: anything that offers an MCP server. Agents read freely, every change waits for your ✅.

Voice

A conversation agent for Assist, Home Assistant's text-to-speech (Piper too, local and free) or ElevenLabs. Simple commands are instant.

Deadlines and memory

Deadlines in emails become reminders; the team remembers customers, prices and decisions.

Costs under control

The estimated Claude spend is always in sight; house commands and Assist's simple commands cost nothing.

Everywhere

In your pocket, and for the whole family

On the phone a room's card rises from the bottom: drag it down to close it, up to open the room. Everyone chooses what to see, in their own language.

  • Non-admin usersThey see only the Home view: lights, climate, cameras, alarm with the code. No assistant, mail or costs. The add-on enforces it.
  • Home onlyWhoever just wants to turn on a light switches off HQ, Map and the assistant in the preferences.
  • Italian or EnglishThe dashboard follows each user's Home Assistant language, or the one they pick.
The rules

An assistant that takes no initiative

Nothing without your order

Routines are off until you turn them on. No action in the house without your request; locks and the alarm ask for confirmation or the code.

Every change goes through you

Emails, data in your work programs, sensitive actions: first a proposal with ✅ / ❌, then it runs, once.

Your data stays at home

Configuration, floor plan and memory live in your Home Assistant and go into its backups. The repository holds nothing of yours.

Open

Open source under the MIT licence. Every part switches on when you need it: you start with just the Anthropic key.

Getting started

Three minutes to turn it on

  1. Home Assistant → Settings → Add-ons → Add-on store → ⋮ → Repositories: add https://github.com/P1pp89/HubNEX.
  2. Install HubNEX, enter the Anthropic API key, start it and turn on "Show in sidebar".
  3. Open HubNEX, draw your floor plan and tell the assistant who you are. Telegram, mail, voice, energy and connectors can be added whenever you like.
